 AI-Powered Project Management & Workflow Automation

An AI-powered platform that streamlines construction and business operations through intelligent agents, automated communication, and multi-project management. AI agents handle client email enquiries, generate responses, review CC recipients and meeting attendees for relevance, and match potential client requirements with historical blueprints and designs for reuse with necessary modifications. Integrated with WhatsApp, Gmail, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Calendar APIs, the platform provides role-based access for clients, employees, finance, insurance, and project managers, alongside live project tracking and updates.

The platform also features a **Four-Eyes Approval Protocol** for critical processes, including financial, insurance, construction, and communication workflows. Project managers can oversee subordinates and monitor multiple projects, while AI agents automate reviews, flag decisions requiring human approval, and escalate unanswered approvals after two hours. The goal is to reduce administrative workload, improve efficiency, and ensure accountability and transparency across every project.
